The reactive database you know.
With the cap removed.

CLA DB (@clatech/db) is a minimal Apache 2.0 fork of RxDB, anchored on the RxDB 17.6.0 codebase (commit 9d5849cf). It keeps the reactive API, the Sync Engine, and the on-disk format, and removes the artificial collection limit. Deltas are small, isolated, and marked with // CLA-DB: comments so upstream merges stay clean.
